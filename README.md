## 🎮 About the Game

Garden of Witches is a single-player action roguelike developed and published by Team Tapas. It fully launched on August 28, 2026, after entering Early Access on May 23, 2025. Players control Sil, the Thread Witch, using giant scissors and witchcraft to defeat enemies, confront other witches, and save the Garden.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Garden of Witches**. This trainer enhances Garden of Witches runs by providing control over survivability, combat resources, cooldowns, movement, and build progression. It is designed around the game's Scissors, Spells, Special Skills, Imprints, Relics, permanent upgrades, and Challenge Mode systems.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to experiment with Scissors, Spells, Imprints, Relics, and Challenge Mode builds, practice witch attack patterns, reduce repetition between runs, or make the campaign more accessible.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Infinite Health** — Prevents Sil's health from decreasing during encounters, allowing safer exploration and boss attempts.
- **Rapid Movement** — Increases Sil's movement speed for quicker traversal and more responsive positioning in arena combat.
- **Easy Build Progression** — Supports faster access to run upgrades, Imprints, Relics, and other progression choices during a run.
- **Permanent Upgrade Support** — Helps players make more efficient use of upgrade progression available at Sil's House between runs.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Unlimited Dashes** — Removes dash restrictions so Sil can reposition more freely and use the dash's invincibility frames more often.
- **No Skill Cooldowns** — Allows Scissors, Spells, and Special Skills to be used without waiting for their normal cooldowns.
- **Unlimited Sharpness** — Maintains the Sharpness resource used by Scissors builds, supporting consistent weapon damage and critical-hit effects.
- **Maximum Damage** — Boosts outgoing damage from Scissors, Spells, and Special Skills for faster room clears and boss fights.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Launch Garden of Witches and load into gameplay before activating movement options. Use Rapid Movement for faster navigation through rooms, and use Unlimited Dashes when you need frequent repositioning or additional protection during enemy attacks. Disable movement enhancements before interacting with menus or scripted events if movement behaves unexpectedly.

### Tools & Inventory

The game organizes combat equipment through Sil's Armory and Book, including Scissors, Spells, Special Skills, Relics, Curses, Runes, and Imprints. Progression-related options should be activated only after entering a run or opening the relevant equipment screen. Avoid changing values while rewards are being generated or while switching between equipment pages.

### World & Environment

Garden of Witches uses repeated runs through a hand-drawn magical world, with combat rooms, rewards, witch encounters, and permanent progression between attempts. Use Infinite Health and Rapid Movement for exploration, then disable unnecessary options before story scenes, chapter transitions, or Challenge Mode loading screens.

### Combat & Weapons

Activate Infinite Health before entering difficult rooms or boss encounters. Unlimited Dashes improves access to the dash's invincibility frames, while No Skill Cooldowns enables frequent use of Spells and Special Skills. Unlimited Sharpness is especially useful for Scissors-focused builds, and Maximum Damage can shorten fights. Disable combat boosts when testing normal balance or learning enemy attack patterns.
